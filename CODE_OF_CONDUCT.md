# Code of Conduct

This hackathon is meant to be a welcoming, collaborative space for everyone in Azerbaijan's developer community.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat all participants, mentors, and organizers with respect.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Credit others' ideas and contributions fairly.
- Keep discussions and content appropriate for a professional, public event.

## Not Acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive language or imagery of any kind.
- Plagiarizing another team's or an external source's code without attribution.
- Sabotaging or interfering with another team's repository or work.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code of conduct, please reach out to the Google Developers Group (GDG) coordinators supporting the hackathon — in person, or through whatever communication channel is shared with participants at the start of the event.

Violations may result in disqualification from judging.
